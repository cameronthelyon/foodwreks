{{BRAND}} vs Tock

A Tock alternative for restaurants that take reservations, not tickets

Tock is built for prepaid experiences: tasting menus, ticketed dinners, events. If that is your business, Tock does something {{BRAND}} does not. If you mostly take ordinary reservations, you are paying a subscription and prepayment fees for tools you do not use.

Where Tock is stronger

  • Prepaid and ticketed experiences. Selling a tasting menu in advance, a winemaker dinner, a holiday seating with a set price. {{BRAND}} does not take prepayments today. It holds a card and can charge a no-show fee, which is a different thing.
  • Takeout and events tools alongside reservations.

Where {{BRAND}} is different

  • Built for the nightly book. Smart seating, pacing, a live floor timeline, walk-ins and a texting waitlist, without an experiences platform wrapped around it.
  • No subscription and no percentage. Card holds run on your own Stripe account; we take nothing from them.
  • Not part of a merger. Your reservation system will not change hands or change plans because of a corporate integration. Open source and being formed as a cooperative. How that works

Who should stay, who should switch

Stay if prepaid tasting menus or ticketed events are a large part of your revenue.

Switch if you mainly take regular reservations and walk-ins, and use card holds only to discourage no-shows.
