What "lifetime" means, exactly

  • Per location, for as long as you operate it. Two locations, two licenses. Each runs on its own.
  • It transfers if you sell. The new owner keeps the book, the guests and the license. (Texting registration is tied to the business's tax ID, so a new owner re-registers.)
  • Every update, forever. There is no premium tier to upgrade to.
  • Your data leaves whenever you want. Guests, reservations and history export as CSV or JSON in one click, any day, even after a trial ends.
  • The code is open source (AGPL-3.0). If {{BRAND}} ever stopped operating, the software would still exist for you or anyone you hire to run it.

How one payment can last

Because the software is built to cost almost nothing to run. One modest server handles thousands of restaurants. There is no sales team, no marketplace to advertise, and no investor waiting for a toll on every cover. A fixed share of every license goes into a reserve that pays for hosting and upkeep over time, like a small endowment. The costs that do grow with use, texting and hands-on help, are the ones we price separately and say so up front.

The trial

30 days, every feature, no card. If you do not buy, online booking pauses at the end of the trial. Your staff keep access to the book and you can export everything. Nothing is deleted.
