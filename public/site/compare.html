When {{BRAND}} is the wrong choice

If a big share of your tables come from people browsing a marketplace app, or you sell prepaid tasting menus and tickets, or you need one dashboard across many locations, an incumbent may serve you better today. {{BRAND}} is for independent restaurants whose guests already find them on Google, Instagram, their own site and by word of mouth, and who are tired of paying a toll or rent on those guests.
